About Our Company

Building the Future with Precision

Established in 2015 by Mahlangu Victor Themba, Sakha Ithemba Trading is a proudly B-BBEE Level 1 company born from a deep passion for engineering excellence.

Our foundation is built on a Qualification in Instrument Mechanician Trade Test Certificate (Red Seal), obtained in March 2010. Today, we bring approximately 20 years of combined experience across multiple high-demand industries, delivering uncompromising quality and safety.
